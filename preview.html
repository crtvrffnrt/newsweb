Check Point / CVE-2026-16232 · SmartConsole

SmartConsole authentication bypass exploited against exposed management servers

Check Point confirmed in-the-wild exploitation of a SmartConsole login flaw that can let an unauthenticated remote attacker obtain an application token and authenticate with full administrative privileges. The exposed control plane can be used to modify security policies and management configuration.

Recommended action

Install the latest Jumbo Hotfix, restrict Trusted Clients and management access to approved IP ranges, and review the published source IP indicators and recent administrative changes.

Cisco Talos / Chaos / msaRAT · Browser-mediated C2

Chaos ransomware hides RAT command-and-control inside Chrome and Edge traffic

Cisco Talos identified a Rust-based RAT that launches Chrome or Edge and controls it through the Chrome DevTools Protocol. Command-and-control traffic then leaves through legitimate browser processes using Cloudflare-hosted infrastructure, Google STUN and Twilio TURN over WebRTC.

Recommended action

Hunt for unusual browser launches with remote-debugging behavior, loopback-only malware communication, unexpected WebRTC sessions and browser traffic to newly observed Cloudflare developer domains.

Origin Energy / Customer data breach

Origin Energy confirms unauthorized access and disclosure of customer data

Origin Energy confirmed that customer records were accessed and disclosed without authorization. Potentially affected fields include names, addresses, dates of birth, phone numbers, account information and partial credit-card or bank-account digits.

Recommended action

Prepare targeted phishing and identity-abuse monitoring, notify affected users through verified channels, and watch for credential stuffing or impersonation attempts using exposed account context.
